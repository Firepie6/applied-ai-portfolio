## Useful verification conversations

For the deployed work, a reviewer can ask for a redacted walkthrough, a discussion of required fields and exception handling, and employer-verifiable responsibilities. Those materials should be prepared with permission before sharing internal operational data.

For the portfolio lab, the logic and tests are public. Its staff authorization is an explicit transition, and its data is synthetic. This is a separate implementation, not a claim about stronger controls in the original platform.

No private records, credentials, messages, photographs or employer application source are required to inspect the public artifacts.
